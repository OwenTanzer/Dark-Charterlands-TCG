### Balance proposal record

Use one record per hypothesis. Copy its finding into the shared findings table
and its candidate action into the shared proposals table. Example format:
"Card [ID/revision] seemed too strong/weak because [hypothesis]; in game [ID],
turn [N], [observable event, E#]; try [specific candidate]; retest against
[comparison or success criterion]."
